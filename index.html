🧠 BrainChallenge Publisher

Think smart. Play hard. Challenge your mind.

BrainChallenge creates short quiz and brain-teaser videos.

BrainChallenge Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by the BrainChallenge creator. When a new video is ready, the tool uploads it to the BrainChallenge YouTube channel and the BrainChallenge Facebook Page and schedules it for publication.

The tool is not offered to the public, and it only accesses the creator's own channel and Page. It does not access, collect or store data from any other user.
