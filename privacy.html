Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. What this app is

BrainChallenge Publisher ("the app") is a private, internal tool used only by the creator of BrainChallenge. It uploads BrainChallenge quiz videos to the creator's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page and schedules them for publication. The app is not offered to the public.

2. Data the app accesses

The app only accesses the account of its owner, after the owner explicitly authorizes it:

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel information, such as the channel ID and name (youtube.readonly). This information is used only to confirm that videos go to the correct channel.
  • Facebook (Meta): permission to list the Pages the owner manages and to publish and schedule videos (Reels) on the BrainChallenge Page.

The only content the app sends to these platforms is the owner's own videos, together with their titles, descriptions and hashtags.

3. Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of viewers, followers or any other user. It does not read comments, messages, subscriber lists or analytics about other people.

4. Storage and security

The access tokens issued by Google and Meta are stored only on the owner's private server, in a configuration file that only the owner can read. They are never published, shared or sent to third parties.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data obtained through Google APIs is not used for advertising and is not transferred to anyone.

6. Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; by using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google/YouTube: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Facebook: go to Settings → Business Integrations and remove the app.

Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the server.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/brainchallengee-bit/brainchallenge-legal/issues.
